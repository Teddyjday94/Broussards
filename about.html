About us

A father and son business in Sorrento, Louisiana.

Mike Broussard got his start in 1990, with a year and a half at Nicholls State University. He then went through a four-year apprenticeship with Local 198 in HVAC and industrial refrigeration, working summers along the way.

He earned his Louisiana Mechanical license in 1996. In 2006 he passed further exams and opened Broussard's Cooling & Heating on Highway 44 in Sorrento. He has kept up with continuing education every year since.

Today his son John Michael works alongside him as a partner. When you call, you talk to one of the two people who will actually come out and do the work.
