A/C quit? Check these first.

Four quick checks that sometimes save you a service call.

ThermostatSet to COOL and below room temperature. A blank screen usually means dead batteries.

BreakersCheck the indoor and outdoor breakers. If one trips again, leave it off and call us.

Air filterA clogged filter can freeze the coil. In our humidity, change it every 1 to 3 months.

Drain lineWater in the drain pan can trip the float switch and shut the whole system down.

Common questions

Do you work on my brand of equipment?

Most likely. We service most makes and models of central air, heat pumps, furnaces and ductless systems. For new installs we offer American Standard, Daikin, Bosch and Mitsubishi Electric.

How often should my A/C be serviced in Louisiana?

Twice a year is ideal: once in spring before the heat and once in fall before you switch to heat. Systems here run almost year-round, so they wear faster than they do up north.

My system still uses R-22 freon. Should I worry?

R-22 was phased out in 2020, so what’s left is limited and expensive. If an R-22 system develops a leak, replacing it is often the better long-term choice. We’ll go over both options honestly.

Do you give estimates for new systems?

Yes. We’ll look at your home, size the system properly and walk you through options and prices before any work begins.

Do you do commercial work?

We do. We’re licensed for commercial mechanical work and handle service, maintenance and installs for local businesses.
