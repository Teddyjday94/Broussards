A/C stopped working?

Before you call, check these. Sometimes they're all it takes.

  1. Thermostat. Make sure it's set to cool and below the room temperature. If the screen is blank, try new batteries.
  2. Breakers. Check the breakers for the inside and outside units. If one trips again after you reset it, leave it off and call us.
  3. Air filter. A dirty filter can freeze up the coil. With our humidity, change it every one to three months.
  4. Drain line. If there's water standing in the drain pan, a safety switch may have shut the system off.

Should you repair it or replace it?

A rough rule: if the system is under 10 years old, a repair usually makes sense. If it's over 12 to 15 years, has needed more than one repair lately, or still runs on R-22 freon, it's worth getting a price on a new one before you put more money into it. We'll give you both numbers and let you decide.

Questions people ask us

Do you work on my brand?

Most likely, yes. We service most makes and models of central air, heat pumps, furnaces and ductless systems.

How often should my A/C be serviced?

Twice a year is best here: in spring before the heat, and in fall before you switch over to heat. Systems in south Louisiana run almost all year, so they wear out faster.

My system uses R-22 freon. Is that a problem?

R-22 was phased out in 2020, so what's left is limited and expensive. If an R-22 system starts leaking, replacing it is often the better deal. We'll price out both options for you.

Do you charge for estimates on new systems?

No. We'll come look at the house, figure out the right size, and go over options and prices before anything is done.
